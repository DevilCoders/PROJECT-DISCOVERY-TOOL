# Re-audit of 2026-11-12: started-but-unfinished and broken parts

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Code audited:** `65dc3e2` (the head of `feat/discovery-engine-foundation`) · **Summary and register entries:** [AUDIT.md §12](AUDIT.md#12-re-audit-of-2026-11-12)

Eleven auditors each read one subsystem end to end, with its tests and documentation, and were told to prove each candidate defect with a script or command, or to cite the exact code when they could not run it. Seven finished: `inventory-engine`, `cli-reporting`, `code-intelligence`, `deps-vulns`, `online-repos`, `plugins`, `catalog-packaging-docs`. The session's usage limit stopped the other four (`security-analyzers`, `domain-analyzers-a`, `domain-analyzers-b` and the black-box `e2e` run) before they reported. They were audited in a [second round](#second-round) against `2367023`, after the first round's fixes.

**These findings are unverified.** At the owner's request no independent verification agents were run; the owner verifies the findings. A finding marked Fixed was confirmed while fixing it (by a reproduction or by reading the code) and has a regression test. Open findings are reported as the auditor wrote them, except that credential- and address-shaped examples are redacted (`<credentials>@`, `<user>@<host>`).

124 findings: 4 P0, 4 P1, 62 P2, 54 P3. Severity: P0 core feature broken · P1 security of PDT itself · P2 correctness, precision or operability · P3 minor.
